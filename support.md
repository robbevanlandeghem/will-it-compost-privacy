# Will it compost? Support

Will it compost? tells you whether something can go on your compost heap at home, and helps you keep track of your piles.

## Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: email [willitcompost@outlook.com](mailto:willitcompost@outlook.com). When reporting a bug, please mention your phone model and what you were doing when it happened.

## Common questions

**An item I searched for isn't in the app.**
Tap **Ask us to add it** under the search results. That sends what you typed, so we know which items to add next. See the [privacy policy](./) for exactly what is sent.

**Does a "yes" mean it can go in my municipal food waste bin?**
No. Every verdict is for a compost heap or bin at home. Your local collection may have different rules.

**How do reminders work?**
Turn on **Reminders** in Settings. You get a nudge when a pile is due a turn: two weeks after the last turn, then weekly. There are no reminders in winter. If you don't get any, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings.

**How do I change the language?**
Go to Settings and tap **Language**. The app is available in English and Dutch.

**Is my data backed up?**
Your piles and settings are stored only on your phone, and in your phone's own backups if you use them. We don't have a copy, so deleting the app deletes them.
