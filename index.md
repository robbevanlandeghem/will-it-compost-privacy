# Will it compost? Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what the Will it compost? app does with your data. It applies to the app on iOS, Android and macOS.

The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your piles and settings stay on your device. Data only leaves your device when you tap **Ask us to add it** after a search that found nothing, and even then it can't be traced back to you.

## Who is responsible

Robbe Van Landeghem, Belgium, makes Will it compost? and is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For any question about privacy or this policy, email [willitcompost@outlook.com](mailto:willitcompost@outlook.com).

## Data that stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

- Your piles: their names, the items you added to them, and the dates you turned each pile.
- Your language setting.
- Whether pile-turn reminders are on.

Reminders are notifications that your device schedules and shows by itself. Nothing is sent to set them up or show them.

What you type in the search box is not saved or sent, unless you ask us to add an item (see below).

The only exception: if you ask for an item while offline, the request waits on your device until it can be sent.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

## Data sent when you ask for an item

When a search finds nothing, you can tap **Ask us to add it**. Only then is the following sent to our analytics provider, Aptabase:

| Data | Example | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you searched for, in lowercase | "pizza box" | You typed it |
| The app's language | English | Your setting in the app |
| Your device's operating system and its version | iOS 26.1 | The app |
| Your device's language and region setting | nl-BE | The app |
| The app's version and build number | 1.0.0 (1) | The app |
| A random session code | a code that renews after an hour without requests | The app |
| Your approximate location | Belgium, Flanders | Worked out by Aptabase from your IP address |
| A daily visitor code | a scrambled code that changes every day | Worked out by Aptabase from your IP address |

Your IP address is only used for a moment to work out your country and region and the daily visitor code. It is never stored. The daily visitor code is a one-way scrambled value (a hash) made with a secret that is thrown away after a day, so it can't be turned back into your IP address or linked across days.

The app does not send your name, email address, contacts, precise location, advertising ID or any other device identifier. Because what you type is sent as-is, please don't type personal information into a search you ask us about.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

We use these requests for one purpose: deciding which items to add to the app next. The language, country and region tell us which list needs the item, for example the Dutch one. The device details help us check that requests come through correctly.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). You give it each time you tap **Ask us to add it**, after the note under the button tells you what is sent. Not tapping it means nothing is sent, and the app works exactly the same.

We never sell the data, use it for advertising, build profiles or combine it with other data.

## Who receives it

Requests go to [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy), an analytics service run by Technov Solutions SRL in Romania. Aptabase only processes the data on our behalf, under a [data processing agreement](https://aptabase.com/legal/dpa) (GDPR article 28). That agreement binds Aptabase to protect the data at least as well as this policy does.

Aptabase uses two subprocessors: Tinybird Data S.L. (Spain) for its database and Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL for hosting. All data is processed and stored in the European Union, on Aptabase's EU servers in Germany. It is not transferred outside the EU.

No one else receives the data.

## How long we keep it

- **Requests** are kept by Aptabase for up to 5 years, then deleted automatically.
- **Your IP address** is not kept at all. The secret used to make the daily visitor code is deleted within 48 hours.
- **Data on your device** stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app.

We can't delete one person's requests on demand. Nothing in them tells us who sent them, so we can't find yours.

## Security

Requests are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS with TLS) and stored encrypted at Aptabase. Each app's data is kept separate, and access to Aptabase's systems requires multi-factor authentication. The app has no accounts or passwords that could leak.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email [willitcompost@outlook.com](mailto:willitcompost@outlook.com).

In practice, we can only act on requests if you can show us which ones are yours. Because they can't be linked to you, we usually can't (GDPR article 11).

**Withdrawing consent:** you can stop at any time by not tapping **Ask us to add it**. Requests already sent can't be traced back to you, so they stay until they are deleted after 5 years.

**Complaints:** if you think we handle your data wrongly, please email us first. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Belgium, that is the [Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be) ([lodge a complaint](https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be/burger/acties/klacht-indienen)). If you live elsewhere in the EU, you can complain to the authority in your own country.

## Children

The app is suitable for everyone and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children. Requests can't be linked to a person, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this policy and its date before the change ships. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
